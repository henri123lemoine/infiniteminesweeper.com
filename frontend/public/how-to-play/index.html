How to Play Infinite Minesweeper

Infinite Minesweeper is classic Minesweeper on a board with no edges, shared live by everyone who is online. There is no game over and no timer: you explore an endless minefield alongside other players, earn points for every safe cell you open and every mine you flag, and compete on a global leaderboard. It's free, runs in your browser, and needs no download or account.

Play now

The basics

If you've played Minesweeper before, you already know the core rule: every revealed number tells you how many of its eight neighbouring cells hide a mine. Use those numbers to work out which hidden cells are safe to open and which ones to flag.

  • Revealing an empty cell (a zero) automatically opens the whole surrounding area.
  • Flags can only be placed within two cells of something already revealed, so you always work outward from known territory.
  • Hitting a mine doesn't end your game. It costs points and resets your streak, and the exploded mine stays on the board for everyone to see.

Chording only works once a number has exactly as many flags (or exploded mines) around it as its value. If one of those flags is wrong, chording will set off the mine it was hiding, so be sure first.

A world without edges

The board stretches forever in every direction. It is generated from a fixed seed in 64×64-cell chunks as players explore, so it never runs out and every player sees exactly the same mines.

Mine density changes smoothly from region to region, from calm areas with hardly any mines to brutal sectors where half the cells or more are mines. Around one cell in five is a mine on average. Open the minimap to see the whole explored world and jump anywhere in it.

Playing together

Everyone online plays on the same board at the same time. You see other players' reveals and flags appear live, each in their own flag design. The home screen shows the current hotspot, where the most players are active, and the leaderboard ranks everyone by total score.

Every value is multiplied by the sector multiplier, and rewards are also boosted by your flawless streak. Your total score never drops below zero.

Sector multiplier (up to 15×)

  • Difficulty: denser sectors are worth more, from 0.2× where mines are rare (10% or less) to 1× at about 21% mines and up to 5× at 50% and above. Penalties scale the same way, so dense sectors are high risk, high reward.
  • Crowd bonus: playing near other people raises the multiplier, up to 3× when a dozen players are active within two chunks of you.

Flawless streak (up to +200%)

Every correct action in a row (a safe reveal or a correct flag) adds 5% to your rewards, up to triple points. Any mistake resets the streak to zero.

Advancements and flags

Advancements reward milestones, and most of them unlock a new flag design to plant on the board. A few examples:

  • Wanderer, Pioneer, Pathfinder: travel ever further from the centre of the world.
  • Steady Hand, Master Sweeper, Nerves of Steel: keep a flawless streak of 150, 1,500 and 7,500 actions.
  • Deminer, Bomb Specialist: place 250 and 2,500 correct flags.
  • Land Surveyor: fully clear a 64×64 chunk.
  • Into the Fire, Danger Seeker: reveal cells in the most mine-dense sectors.

Tips

  • Start at the edge of revealed territory, where the numbers give you the most information.
  • Flag first, then chord: it's faster and earns +10 for every correct flag.
  • Protect your streak. When a cell is a pure guess, leave it for later; new numbers nearby often settle it.
  • Head to the hotspot to share the crowd bonus with other players.
  • Dense sectors pay up to 5× but punish mistakes just as hard. Build your streak somewhere calmer first.

Frequently asked questions

Is Infinite Minesweeper free?
Yes. It's free to play in any modern browser, with no download or sign-up.
Is the board really infinite?
For all practical purposes, yes. The world is generated on demand as people explore, so there is always more board in every direction.
Can I play on my phone?
Yes. Tap to reveal, long-press to flag, drag to move and pinch to zoom.
What happens when I hit a mine?
You lose 100 points times the sector multiplier and your streak resets, but you keep playing. The mine stays exploded on the board.
Do I need an account?
No. Pick a name and a flag and start playing. Your player is remembered in this browser.
Can I play with friends?
Everyone online shares the same world, so you and your friends are always on the same board. Meet up at the hotspot or pick a spot on the minimap.
Is there a game over?
No. There's no timer and no losing screen. The board keeps going and so can you.
